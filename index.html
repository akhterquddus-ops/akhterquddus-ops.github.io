An on-premise AI assistant for electricity utilities. A manager asks about consumers, billing, collection, losses or smart meters in plain English and gets the answer, a chart and the SQL behind it. Utility Rules Copilot · CSM Help Desk

A help desk for DISCO front-desk staff and consumers. Ask the NEPRA Consumer Service Manual in English or Roman Urdu and get a short answer, the exact clause it comes from, and a reply ready to paste into WhatsApp. Exact calculators cover sanctioning officer, connection time frames, transformer rules and security deposit. It runs on a local computer with open-source models.

English and Roman Urdu. Python, Streamlit, Ollama, ChromaDB. Not an official NEPRA product.
